# Claktory

Your own AI team. Say what you want. Your agents get it done.

Claktory is an app on your own computer where AI agents work for you as a team and reach you on your phone when they need you. It's free and uses your Claude or ChatGPT plan.

## Get it

- **Mac** with an M1 chip or newer and macOS 12 or later: [Download](https://claktory.com/download)
- **Linux server** (x86_64 or aarch64): run this on the server, then follow [these steps](https://claktory.com/docs/install-linux).

  ```
  curl -fsSL https://claktory.com/install.sh | bash
  ```

- **iPhone and iPad:** [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6837053917). In the EU, use [TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/kSYkdayX).
- **Android:** [Claktory for Android](https://claktory.com/android)
